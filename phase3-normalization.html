Normalization

Formal normalization: from one unnormalized form to BCNF.

Dependencies and Key
  • sale_id → seller, buyer, date, status.
  • seller_id → name, zone; product_id → name, category, price; category_id → category_name.
  • (sale_id, product_id) → quantity_sold, unit_price_at_sale.
  • The candidate key of the unnormalized form is {sale_id, product_id}.
3 · Normalization
Decomposition Process

UNF (Unnormalized Form) ▸

A sale form with repeating groups of products before any normalization splits are applied.

1NF (First Normal Form) ▸

Ensures atomic values and one row per sale and product combination.

2NF (Second Normal Form) ▸

Removes partial dependencies by splitting into Sales, Products, and Sale_Details tables.

3NF (Third Normal Form) ▸

Removes transitive dependencies by isolating Sellers, Accounts, and Categories.

BCNF (Boyce-Codd Normal Form) ▸

Verifies that every determinant is a superkey across all 12 tables.
3 · Normalization
Proofs
  • Lossless: each split joins on the key of one side (Heath’s theorem).
  • Dependency-preserving: every dependency sits inside one final table.
  • unit_price_at_sale is an intended price snapshot that depends on the sale line, so it is not a violation.
