1 · Proposal
The Problem
  • Producers keep stock, sales and costs in notebooks, so mistakes stay hidden until money runs short.
  • Stock and capital drift apart because nothing checks them.
  • There is no proof of payment, audit trail or cash-flow view for SLP admins.
  • Rural signal is weak, so transactions must be recordable offline.
1 · Proposal
The Solution
  • One normalized database (12 tables, BCNF) is the single source of truth.
  • Triggers and procedures enforce the rules, so the data protects itself.
  • Offline sales sync without duplicates, and every paid sale gets a JSON receipt.
  • Verified inventory and an append-only capital ledger give a full audit trail.
1 · Proposal
Objective and Why It Matters
  • The main objective is to design and prove a rule-enforcing database through modeling, normalization, DDL, triggers, queries and indexing.
  • A spreadsheet cannot refuse a bad entry, but the database rejects negative stock, edited balances and empty paid sales.
  • Every peso or centavos must be traceable, and even any changes or progress offline can be saved while the database stays reliable.
