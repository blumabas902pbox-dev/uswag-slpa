Modeling

Conceptual model: ERD with 1:1, 1:M, and M:N cardinalities in Crow’s Foot notation.

Entities and Cardinality
  • Task: model the domain with explicit 1:1, 1:M, and M:N cardinalities.
  • Result: 12 entities and 16 relationships, generated from the live database.
  • Strong entities: Accounts, Sellers, Categories, Products and Sales.
  • Weak entities: Sale_Details (identified by Sales) and Capital_Ledger (identified by Sellers).
  • Sale_Details acts as an associative entity resolving the M:N relationship between Sales and Products.
  • Optional links (0..1): the buyer on a sale and the verifier on an inventory entry.
2 · Modeling
Business Rules
  • Stock changes only through admin-verified inventory entries.
  • A paid sale credits capital once and issues one receipt, and an empty sale cannot be paid.
  • Expenses debit capital, and every money movement is written to the ledger.
  • Offline sales carry a client UUID, so syncing never creates duplicates.
